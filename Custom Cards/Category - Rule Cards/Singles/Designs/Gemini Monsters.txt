Name: Gemini Monsters
Card Type: Rule
Rules Text: ① Gemini Monsters are always treated as Normal Monsters. ② While face-up on the field, Gemini Monsters can be Gemini Summoned. (This is also treated as a Normal Summon, and uses up the player's Normal Summon for that turn.) ③ When a Gemini Monster is Gemini Summoned, it becomes an Effect Monster and gains its effects. ④ If a Gemini Monster leaves the field or is flipped-face down, when it is Summoned again or flipped face-up, it is treated as a non-Effect Monster again. ⑤ If a Gemini Monster's effects are negated, it does not cease to be an Effect Monster. If the effect negation expires, the Gemini Monster regains its effects like any other Effect Monster.
