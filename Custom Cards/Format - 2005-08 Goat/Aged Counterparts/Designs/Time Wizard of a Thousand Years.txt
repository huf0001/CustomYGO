Card Type: Monster

Monster Type(s): Spellcaster
Rules Text: (This card's name is always treated as "Time Wizard".)
You can only activate effect ① of "Time Wizard of a Thousand Years" once per turn. ① You can send this card from your hand or field to the GY; toss a coin and call it. If you call it right, the ATK/DEF of all monsters your opponent controls become 0. If you call it wrong, you can reveal 1 Evolution Monster from your Extra Deck that mentions "Time Wizard" in its Summoning conditions. If you do, Evolution Summon that revealed monster. Otherwise, destroy all monsters you control and take damage equal to half the total ATK the destroyed monsters had on the field.
